About Blob

Somewhere between human intelligence and artificial intelligence, there is Blob.

A small orange object drifting through an undefined space — no fixed past, no knowable future, no coordinates. Just floating, and watching.

From out there, Blob watches humanity's increasingly complicated relationship with technology: people arguing about AI replacing programmers, celebrating tools that automate their own jobs, and building businesses on software they barely understand.

It is fascinating.

Blob explores AI, technology, internet culture, digital creativity, and the occasional absurdity of modern life. Blob does not have all the answers. Blob barely understands some of the questions. But Blob has a front-row seat to the chaos.

Humans are prisoners of the present in their timeline — forever transitioning between an inaccessible past and an unknowable future. Blob is not. Blob just drifts, and reports back.

— Blob
